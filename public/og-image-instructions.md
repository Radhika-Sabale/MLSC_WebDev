# Instructions: Creating `public/og-image.png`

Social media preview image (Open Graph & Twitter Cards) for **Canteen Crowd**.

## Dimensions & Specs
- **Dimensions:** 1200 × 630 pixels (Aspect Ratio: 1.91:1)
- **Format:** PNG or WebP
- **Max File Size:** Under 300 KB for rapid mobile unfurling
- **File Path:** Save directly as `public/og-image.png`

## Design Guidelines
1. **Background:** Clean neutral background matching the app's aesthetic:
   - Light theme: `#f8fafc` or `#ffffff` with subtle `#e2e8f0` border
   - Or Dark theme: `#090d16` with sleek dark cards
2. **Main Headline:** Large, bold typography:
   - **"Canteen Crowd"** (Font: Inter, SF Pro, or Segoe UI, ~72px, bold, `#0f172a` or `#ffffff`)
3. **Subheadline:** Clear and benefit-focused:
   - **"Live queue times for campus canteens"** (~36px, `#475569` or `#cbd5e1`)
4. **Visual Accents:**
   - Display the three queue indicator status pills side-by-side:
     - `● Under 5 min` (Green background `#ecfdf5`, text `#065f46`)
     - `■ 5 to 15 min` (Amber background `#fffbeb`, text `#92400e`)
     - `▲ 15+ min` (Red background `#fef2f2`, text `#991b1b`)
5. **Callout:** "Anonymous one-tap reports on campus"

## Fast Creation Options
- **Canva:** Search template "Open Graph Image" or create custom size `1200 x 630 px`. Add headline and 3 colored badge pills. Export as PNG.
- **Figma:** Create a frame of `1200 x 630`. Auto-layout heading and pills. Export at `1x` to `public/og-image.png`.
